## 4. 6-Step Judge Demonstration Walkthrough (AC1 → AC6)

Follow this sequence during evaluation to prove the complete real-time pipeline:

### Step 1 — Real Video & Stable ByteTrack IDs (AC1 & AC3)
1. Open `http://localhost:5173/` in your browser.
2. In the **Live Monitoring** grid, observe **`CAM-01`** streaming live MJPEG footage.
3. Observe **real bounding boxes** burned directly into the stream by the backend YOLOv8n + ByteTrack pipeline.
4. Note that track IDs (e.g. `1`, `2`, `3`) follow moving targets smoothly without stuttering or client-side coordinate drift.

### Step 2 — 24/7 Live Camera & Webcam Ingestion (AC2)
1. Click **`[+ ADD CAMERA]`** in the top bar.
2. Switch to the **`WEBCAM (LIVE 24/7)`** tab and enter Device Index `0` (or your connected USB camera).
3. Click **`REGISTER & START PERCEPTION`**.
4. The webcam tile appears immediately on the grid and begins continuous real-time perception.

### Step 3 — In-Place Polygon Zone & Tripwire Geofencing (AC4)
1. On any camera card, click **`[ZONE]`** or **`[TRIPWIRE]`** in the upper-right.
2. Click directly on the video feed to draw vertices over a restricted corridor (e.g. crossing path).
3. Click **`[SAVE]`**. The rule immediately activates on the backend `ZoneMonitor`.
4. When a tracked person/vehicle crosses the boundary, a **real alert** triggers instantly over WebSocket into the **`LIVE PERIMETER ALERTS`** drawer on the right.

### Step 4 — Incident Investigation & Chronological Timeline (AC5)
1. Click **`ALERTS & INCIDENTS`** in the top navigation.
2. Select any logged incident from the queue.
3. Review the discrete chronological event timeline, duration, and captured full-resolution snapshot evidence.
4. Enter an operator disposition note (e.g., *"QRF Patrol dispatched"*) and click **`LOG NOTE`**.

### Step 5 — Grounded AI Operator Assistant
1. Click **`AI OPERATOR ASSISTANT`** in the top navigation.
2. Click any tested query (e.g., *"What targets crossed the perimeter boundary?"* or *"Show dwell time for Track 1"*).
3. Review the strict 3-tier grounded output:
   - **`[OBSERVED FACTS]`**: Exact SQLite disk rows.
   - **`[DETERMINISTIC RULE EVALUATION]`**: Spatial point-in-polygon & tripwire rule outcome.
   - **`[GROUNDED AI SYNTHESIS]`**: Natural-language operational briefing.
4. Try asking for a person's facial identity or weapon status to observe the **Anti-Hallucination Guardrail Refusal Notice**.

### Step 6 — Cryptographic Chain-of-Custody Forensics
1. Click **`EVIDENCE & FORENSICS`** in the top navigation.
2. Click **`RUN INTEGRITY AUDIT`** to verify all SHA-256 Merkle-style root hashes across the SQLite database.
3. Click **`EXPORT JSON`** or **`EXPORT CSV`** to export verifiable audit proof for judicial chain of custody.

## 5. Architectural Invariants

- **Server-Side Annotated MJPEG**: `/api/stream/video/{camera_id}` burns bounding boxes on the backend once per frame and fans out to all connected clients.
- **Single Source Owner**: `CameraWorker` owns the capture adapter to prevent frame theft and FPS degradation.
- **Persist-Before-Publish**: Every event is committed to SQLite WAL before WebSocket emission.
- **Zero Fake Overlays**: Every bounding box, track ID, and alert is generated by actual CV inference.
